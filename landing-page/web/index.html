FIELD NOTES · RESEARCH

What the research says
about construction technology.

Short snippets on the methods behind project capture, drawn from published construction research.

01 / DOCUMENTATIONCASE STUDY

Why construction photos need a place and a date

In a hospital construction case, dated and indexed photos let the owner revisit building conditions long after they were covered up.

Deloach, Liu & Kramer · CITC-VII, 2013
02 / AERIALFIELD METHOD

Repeatable drone flights for progress monitoring

Periodic drone imagery paired with a 4D BIM schedule works best when flights are planned and repeatable, so each capture can be compared with the plan.

Jacob-Loyola et al. · Sensors, 2021
03 / REPORTINGVISUALIZATION

Putting planned and built progress in one view

Overlaying a 4D as-planned model on time-lapse site photos makes planned-versus-built progress easier to inspect and discuss.

Golparvar-Fard et al. · Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering, 2009
04 / WALKTHROUGHSYSTEMATIC REVIEW

Where virtual walkthroughs fit in AEC

A review of 41 AEC studies found virtual walkthroughs used for inspection, facility management, training and design review, most useful where flat plans fall short.

Wen & Gheisari · Construction Innovation, 2020
05 / SPATIALINFRASTRUCTURE CASE

Using terrestrial laser scans to track site conditions

On Chicago's Wacker Drive reconstruction, 23 laser scans along a 0.4-mile corridor documented the work, checked bridge clearance and tracked progress.

Zhang & Arditi · Infrastructures, 2020
