About us · Founded 2025

Intelligence
Visuals.

Capture IV is a construction visual-intelligence platform, founded and launched in 2025 to give every project one clear, secure visual record—and to lead the shift away from scattered photos, lost footage and buried email threads.

Who we are

A new home for
what the site shows.

Capture IV brings aerial drone footage, immersive 360° walkthroughs, site photos and project documents into a single organized record—each file tied to its project, capture date and location. Teams, owners and clients open the same clear view, while the project team keeps full control of who sees what.

Why we founded Capture IV in 2025

More cameras.
Less clarity.

Construction has never captured more—drones, 360° cameras, phones and scanners all produce imagery every day—yet it scatters across devices, inboxes and drives. We founded and launched Capture IV in 2025 to lead a different standard: a connected, secure project record where every capture is organized, understandable and safely shared, from first flight to final handover.

The value we provide

What a clear visual
record is worth.

Capture IV turns everyday site imagery into a decision-ready record—so teams spend less time hunting for files and more time acting on what they show.

01 / ONE RECORD

One organized project record

Every drone flight, walkthrough, photo and document filed by project, date and site area—so the whole visual history is one click away instead of scattered across devices and inboxes.

02 / VISIBILITY

Progress you can actually see

Each flight path drawn over the site map, date-to-date comparison and immersive 360° walkthroughs give owners and teams a shared, accurate view of how the work is moving.

03 / SECURITY

Sharing you fully control

Share a project through a unique, expiring link and access code—or approved Google sign-in—with read-only viewing and data held in an isolated, access-controlled database you can revoke at any time.

04 / EVIDENCE

Fewer disputes, faster decisions

Dated, location-tied visual evidence supports progress reviews, RFIs, change orders and pay applications—and settles questions with a record everyone can trust.

05 / TEAM

Your whole team, organized

Admin and member roles, per-project access and client invitations are managed from one panel, so each person only ever sees the work that’s theirs.

06 / LIFECYCLE

From first capture to handover

One continuous record for the life of the project means the context behind every decision stays available long after the work is done.

Subsurface scanning & underground mapping hardware

Keep the unseen
part of the record.

Organize specialist investigation and inspection deliverables alongside the wider construction record.

High-Frequency Concrete GPR Scanners

Handheld radar workflows for investigating shallow rebar, post-tension cables and conduits within concrete slabs.

Cart-Based Multi-Channel Utility GPR

Wheeled ground-penetrating radar workflows for investigating outdoor utilities and voids. Detection depth varies with ground conditions and the target.

Electromagnetic (EM) Locators

Induction-based locating workflows for tracing conductive utilities and energized cables where suitable signal conditions exist.

Robotic CCTV Pipeline Crawlers

Remote-controlled cameras for collecting visual inspection records inside stormwater and sewer pipelines.

SLAM Mobile Underground LiDAR

GPS-denied handheld or vehicle-mounted scanning for mapping tunnels, mines, vaults and other enclosed spaces.

These specialist workflows describe the planned scope of deliverable support and services. Hardware supply, scanning services, automated measurements and named software integrations are not currently included in the live portal. Availability and supported formats will be confirmed for each project. Third-party names describe the workflows, not an affiliation or existing integration.
