We want Capture IV to be usable by everyone who works on or follows a construction project, including people who use assistive technology.

What we do

  • Pages include a "Skip to main content" link, labelled form fields and buttons, and keyboard-operable controls.
  • We test changes against common accessibility guidance and fix issues we find.

Known limitations

Some map, drawing and video features are highly visual and may be harder to use with a screen reader or keyboard alone. Where you need project information in another format, your project team or our support team can help.

Feedback

If something is difficult to use, tell us at {{ legal.contact_email }} with the page and what you were trying to do. We aim to respond and work on a fix or an alternative.
