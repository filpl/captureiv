Monthly Basic

Monthly payments are not refunded automatically, including for a partly used month, except where the law requires a refund or we approve an exception.

Annual Basic

  • A company administrator can submit an early-cancellation and refund-review request from Billing in the company account.
  • Each request is reviewed by Capture IV staff, who may approve a full or partial refund, decline it, or ask for more information. No prorated formula or refund is guaranteed.
  • Submitting a request does not cancel the subscription, pause access or issue a refund by itself. Cancelling renewal is a separate step.
  • Approved refunds are paid back through Stripe to the original payment method.
