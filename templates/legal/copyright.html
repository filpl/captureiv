Upload only what you have rights to

Companies may upload and share only footage, photos, maps and documents they own or have permission to use. Before sharing, check client and project confidentiality requirements and any rules about recording people or private property.

Reporting infringing content

If you believe material on Capture IV infringes your copyright, send a written notice to {{ legal.contact_email }} with:

  1. your name and contact details, including an email address;
  2. a description of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed;
  3. where the material appears, such as the project link or file name, if you know it;
  4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent or the law;
  5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner;
  6. your physical or electronic signature.

We review notices and may remove or disable access to material and notify the company that uploaded it. Company content is private to each company and its invited viewers, so please include enough detail for us to locate it.

Counter-notices

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake or that you have permission to use it, contact us at the same address with your details, the material removed, and why you believe it was removed in error.

Repeat infringement

We may suspend or close accounts that repeatedly upload infringing material.
