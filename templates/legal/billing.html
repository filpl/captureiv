This page explains Capture IV plans, how payment and automatic renewal work, how to cancel, and what happens to your files when a subscription ends.

1 TB means 1,000,000,000,000 bytes of total stored files at any time. It is not a monthly upload allowance. Uploads that would go over your plan's storage are refused. No paid storage add-ons are offered.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe's secure checkout page. Before checkout we show the plan, price, billing interval and these terms, and ask you to agree to automatic recurring billing. We keep a record of that agreement.

Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the price and interval you chose, on the subscription's billing date, until cancelled.

Cancelling

  • A company administrator can cancel from Billing in the company account before the next renewal.
  • Cancellation stops the next renewal. It takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period, and the company keeps paid access until then.
  • A scheduled cancellation can be reversed before the paid period ends, where Stripe allows it.
  • Cancelling does not delete workspace data.

Refunds are covered in the Refund Policy.

After a subscription ends: 90-day file retrieval

  • For 90 days after paid access ends, an active company administrator can sign in and download the company's existing files.
  • During this window uploads, edits, project changes and client sharing are unavailable, and employee accounts and client viewers do not get retrieval access.
  • A security suspension by Capture IV overrides the retrieval window.
  • Download your own copies before the deadline. Retention after the 90 days is not guaranteed.

Enterprise

Enterprise pricing, scope and terms are set in a separate written agreement, which takes priority over this page where they differ.
