This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Capture IV, operated by {{ legal.entity }}, handles, why, and the choices you have. It covers company accounts, employees, client viewers and visitors to the platform.

Information we handle

  • Account details: name, username, work email, company name and role, and a protected (hashed) password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Customer Content: footage, photos, maps, flight paths, site boundaries and documents that companies upload. These can show people, vehicles or property, and may include location data. Each company controls its own Customer Content.
  • Billing details: plan, billing interval, subscription status and payment records. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and are handled by Stripe; we do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Security and activity records: sign-ins, administrative actions, sharing changes and downloads, including the IP address of the request and the time. For limiting repeated sign-in and code attempts we store only a one-way (hashed) value derived from the IP address.
  • Client viewer access: when you open a shared project we record that access was granted in a session cookie (see the Cookie Policy). Client viewers do not create an account.
  • Messages: details you send through enterprise or contact requests.

How we use it

  • to provide the service: accounts, projects, sharing, storage and playback;
  • to keep the service and each company's data secure and separate, and to investigate misuse;
  • to handle subscriptions, billing records and refund requests;
  • to send service email such as verification, password reset, invitations and access-code updates;
  • to meet legal, accounting and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers on the platform.

Service providers

We use providers that process information for us to run the service:

  • Hosting: Render (application and database).
  • File storage: Cloudflare R2, in a private bucket.
  • Payments: Stripe.
  • Email delivery: our email sending provider.
  • Maps: Google Maps, plus map tiles and libraries loaded from OpenStreetMap, Esri and unpkg. When a map loads, your browser contacts these providers directly, so they receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer under equivalent protections.

How long we keep it

Account, billing and security records are kept while an account is active and afterwards as needed for security, billing, legal and accounting purposes. After a paid subscription ends, the company's administrator can download existing files for 90 days, as described in Plans, Billing & Cancellation. We do not promise indefinite retention, and files are not deleted automatically at the end of that window; deletion follows a reviewed process. Companies can ask us to delete their data.

Security

Customer files are stored privately and served only to signed-in members of the owning company, or to client viewers who have that project's link and code. Each company's data is kept separate from other companies'. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at {{ legal.contact_email }} if you notice a problem.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or ask questions about how it is used, by emailing {{ legal.contact_email }}. If you are an employee or client viewer of a company, we may refer requests about that company's Customer Content to the company, since it controls that content. We respond as required by the laws that apply to you.

Children

Capture IV is a business service and is not intended for children.

Changes

We will post updates to this policy here with a new version date, and notify company administrators of material changes.
